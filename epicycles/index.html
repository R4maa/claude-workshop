Epicycles

Draw any closed shape. A Fourier transform splits it into circles that spin at whole-number speeds, and chained tip to tip, they trace your drawing back. Use fewer circles and the shape blurs into the smooth orbit Ptolemy would have drawn.

k is how many times that circle turns per lap; negative k turns clockwise.

Smoothness decides how many circles a shape needs. The Lemniscate has no corners and is within 0.03% of the original at 12 circles. The Square and Star need about 22 to get under 0.1%, because every corner leaves slow-shrinking terms behind. The Heart is the hardest of the five: the sharp dip at its top still leaves about 0.5% error at 22 circles. Your drawing is sampled at 256 points, so with every circle switched on the curve passes through all of them exactly.
