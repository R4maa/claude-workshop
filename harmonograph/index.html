What am I looking at?

Two pendulums swing the pen left and right, two swing it up and down, each slowly losing height. Their speeds are the interval: a fifth is 3:2, so the pen goes round three times sideways for every two up and down.

Drift tunes the second pendulum on each axis a few cents off its partner. The pair beat against each other, so the figure turns inside out as it shrinks. At zero it just shrinks. Ring time is how long the swing takes to fall to 1%.

The sound plays the same four ratios as sine tones, sideways pair on the left, up-and-down pair on the right, so the beating you see is the beating you hear. The pitch is not the pen's speed: the pen moves around 1.5 times a second, the tones sit near 100–500 Hz.
